# Money Copilot AI — 10-second vertical ad

## Route

One PixVerse v6 reference video supplies the live-action and motion base. Exact UI values, prompts, insight copy, end-card text, and the existing Money Copilot AI logo are composited locally so the final ad does not depend on video-model typography. A separate PixVerse Speech 2.8 HD voiceover is mixed in post.

- `site/assets/marketing/chatgpt-report.png` — authentic ChatGPT report layout
- `site/assets/dashboard/money-copilot-ai-promo-light.png` — brand palette and product UI

## Audio plan

Clean generated picture with no native audio. One crisp, trustworthy English voiceover at 1.22x speed, then local timing, gain control, and a short fade. No background music was requested.
