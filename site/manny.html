What Manny does

  • Voice AI Copilot

    Talk naturally with Manny. It listens, understands, and helps in real time.

  • Budget Tracking

    See what’s left, what’s spent, and stay on track with smart budgeting.

  • Smart Alerts

    Get notified about over-budget spending, bills, and unusual activity.

  • Recurring Reminders

    Never miss a payment. Manny reminds you before it’s due.

  • Spending Insights

    Clear insights and trends so you can make better money decisions.

  • Privacy First

    Your data stays private and secure — always under your control.
