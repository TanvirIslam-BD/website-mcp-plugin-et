Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Expense Tracker AI ("we", "us") is a personal AI finance assistant, delivered as a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server, that lets AI assistants like Claude and ChatGPT log, search, and summarize spending on your behalf. This policy explains what data we collect and how we handle it.

1. Data we collect

  • Expense data you or your connected AI assistant submit — amounts, categories, dates, and notes.
  • Account information such as your email address, used for authentication and support.
  • Usage data such as request logs, timestamps, and error diagnostics needed to operate the service reliably.

2. How we use data

  • To store and retrieve your expenses, budgets, and summaries.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the reliability of the service.
  • To respond to support requests.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your expense data to train third-party AI models.

3. Data sharing

We do not share your personal or financial data with third parties except: (a) infrastructure providers strictly necessary to run the service (e.g. hosting, database), under confidentiality obligations, or (b) when required by law.

4. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

5. Security

We use industry-standard measures — encryption in transit, access controls, and least-privilege practices — to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure, and we encourage you to use strong, unique credentials.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by contacting us. Depending on your jurisdiction, additional rights may apply (e.g. GDPR, CCPA).

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date above.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us via our customer support page.
