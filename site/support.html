Documentation

Setup guides and common answers are in the FAQ and on our GitHub profile.

Common topics

  • Connecting to ChatGPT or Claude — follow the step-by-step connection walkthrough on the home page.
  • Billing and plans — manage or change your plan from your account settings, or email us to cancel/downgrade.
  • Data export or deletion — email support to request an export or full account deletion; see our Privacy Policy for details.
  • Bug reports — include the tool call or command you ran, expected vs. actual behavior, and any error message.

Before you contact us

Most setup issues are resolved by re-authorizing the connection in your AI assistant’s settings, or by checking that your assistant supports MCP tool calls. If that doesn’t resolve it, reach out and we’ll help directly.
