Money Copilot AI is a conversational expense tracker and budget assistant. It lets you log purchases, review spending, and manage budgets by chatting with supported AI assistants.

Connect Money Copilot AI to ChatGPT, Claude, or another MCP client. Describe an expense in plain language, and the assistant sends the structured amount, category, and date to your private expense history.

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 per month and includes natural-language expense logging, category and monthly summaries, limited spending history, and support for MCP clients.

Each subscriber's data is isolated, and Money Copilot AI stores only the expenses they choose to log. It does not connect to bank accounts, cards, or financial institutions.

Money Copilot AI works with ChatGPT, Claude, and other clients that support the Model Context Protocol, including Gemini, Cursor, and VS Code.

A supported AI assistant uses its vision capability to extract the vendor, amount, and date from a receipt image. Money Copilot AI then stores and organizes those details.
