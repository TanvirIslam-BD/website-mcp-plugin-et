Because there's no bank linking, the only data recorded is what you explicitly choose to log. Amounts are stored as exact integer values, not sold or used for anything but your own reports.

No — never. Money Copilot AI has no connection to banks, cards, or financial institutions. You log expenses by typing or by uploading a receipt. Your accounts stay completely untouched, which also means nothing to breach.

Any client that speaks the Model Context Protocol — including Claude (desktop, mobile, and web), ChatGPT, Gemini, Cursor, VS Code, and more. One server works across all of them; your expense history stays consistent no matter which assistant you're using.

There's nothing to install locally. Add the server to your AI client with a single connection link (the "Add to Claude" button, or your client's MCP settings), and start logging expenses in your next message. No config files, no servers to run yourself.

The receipt reading is done by your AI assistant's vision capability, which extracts the amount, vendor, and date, then calls the server with those structured details. The server stores and organizes your expenses — the host model handles the image understanding.
